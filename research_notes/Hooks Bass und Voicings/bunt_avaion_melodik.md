# BUNT. and AVAION: melodic and rhythmic DNA of hooks, keys/guitar parts and basslines

> **Scope.** This is research round 2 (melody and rhythm). It builds on `reports/Stilanalyse Fred again AVAION BUNT.md`, takes the harmony, tempo and instrumentation there as given, and reuses them here only as inputs.
> **Source access.** All pages were read through search-engine extracts. Direct page fetches failed with DNS errors on every host I tried (hooktheory.com, nonstop2k.com, ultimate-guitar.com, youtube.com, beatportal.com, wikipedia.org), so every quote below is as it appeared in those extracts. **I could not reach, or find indexed, any note-level transcription of any BUNT. or AVAION part.** No Hooktheory TheoryTab exists for either artist. Musescore, letter-note and kalimba sites have no entries for them. The UG tabs, the Nonstop2k MIDI files and the remake projects are paid or unreadable. **None of the rules below is a transcription.** The quantified rules come from three places, each labelled: computations on the sourced chord loops and tempos (*computed*), tutorials and sample-pack copy (*genre-level*), and qualitative press and interview facts.
> **Conventions.** Scale degrees are relative to the key's tonic; in minor loops 1 is the minor tonic, with ♭3, ♭6 and ♭7. "st" means semitones. The grid has 16 steps per 4/4 bar: step 1 is the downbeat, and steps 3/7/11/15 are the 8th offbeats. A pitch counts as an **avoid note** if it lies a semitone above a chord tone (a ♭9 against it). All other non-chord tones count as consonant extensions: 9, 11 over minor, 6/13 and maj7; "c" marks #11 as a colour tone.

## 1. BUNT. vocal hook: pitch placement, pitch count, hold length, gate rate, roll placement, phrase repetition

### Takeaway
No source documents the pitches, gate rate or roll positions of any BUNT. hook. Four things are documented:
- The "Clouds" hook is one borrowed word ("easy"); its two syllables are stretched into two long "ee" vowels.
- In "Clouds" the full stutter only starts at 0:39, which is about bar 22 at 130 BPM.
- In "Lose It All" the sample comes back after about 8 bars.
- Borrowed vocals are re-timed by −19 % to +61 % but transposed by only −2 to +3 st.

The computed result: in every sourced BUNT. minor loop the minor tonic is either a chord tone or the 9th over every chord. That is why one held vowel can sit over the whole loop without being re-pitched.

**Gate and retrigger mechanics (genre-level, not BUNT.-specific)**
- Melodigging's recipe:
  - Slice a short vocal phrase into **grains of 1/8 to 1/32 notes** and program **syncopated retriggers that lock to the groove**.
  - Use gating, transient shaping and **sample-start modulation** to create percussive onsets.
  - For variety, add formant shifting, **mild pitch jumps of ±3–7 st** and subtle delay throws.
  — [Melodigging](https://www.melodigging.com/genre/stutter-house)
- A one- or two-chord vamp (i–VI or I–♭VII) gives harmonic context without pulling focus from the vocal hook. — genre guide, most likely [Melodigging](https://www.melodigging.com/genre/stutter-house) (attribution in the search extract not isolated)
- Pitching chops more than about 3–4 st starts to sound unnatural ("chipmunk"/"monster"); formant-preserving shifting avoids this. — [The Vocal Market](https://thevocalmarket.com/blogs/how-to/how-to-chop-vocals-like-a-pro-any-daw)
- "Stutter stairs" step down from 1/32 to 1/64 to 1/128 notes. — [Ableton Forum](https://forum.ableton.com/viewtopic.php?t=74606)
- Loopmasters' "Stutter House" pack (125 BPM) says the stutter effects "transform [the vocals] into mesmerising melodies". In other words, the gate pattern itself becomes the melodic rhythm. — [Loopmasters](https://www.loopmasters.com/genres/25-House/products/18114-Stutter-House). Samplestar ships "gated vocal chops". — [Splice](https://splice.com/sounds/packs/samplestar/stutter-melodic-house-and-vocals/samples)
- Vandalism's "Ultra Stutter House Vocals 2" names BUNT. as a reference and contains 33 vocal loops plus 15 MIDI loops. — [MyLoops](https://www.myloops.net/product/ultra-stutter-house-vocals-2). ### Inferences
**A. Hook construction (from Grimy Goods)**
- The "Clouds" hook is **one word with two syllables ("ea" + "sy"), each stretched into a long held tone on the vowel /i/ ("ee")**. That means about **2 pitch events per hook cycle**, one held pitch per syllable, possibly with a glide on the "-a" release of "eeeeeea". On a two-chord loop the natural mapping is one syllable per chord (F, then Am). The construction is sourced; the pitch and chord mapping is my inference (medium confidence).
- If harmonic rhythm is 1 chord per bar, each stretched syllable lasts 1 bar (1.85 s), which is **16 gate steps at 1/16**. At 2 bars per chord it lasts 3.7 s, or 32 steps. Bars per chord are unsourced (see Gaps).
- A synthetic fallback for a Clouds-type hook should emulate the "ee" vowel; the main report gives its formants.

**B. Source-vocal transforms (*computed* from the cited tempos and keys)**

- **Rule (computed):** under the simplest collection mapping, every whole-phrase transposition falls within **±3 st** (−2, −1, +3, +3). Tempo changes span −19 % to +61 %. Varispeed is ruled out in all four cases because it would produce the wrong key each time. A generator therefore needs independent time-stretch, with transposition capped at ±3 st. That cap matches The Vocal Market's 3–4 st naturalness limit. Melodigging's ±3–7 st applies to per-slice jumps inside a chop pattern, not to the whole phrase.
- **Reharmonisation pattern (computed):** in "Clouds", a major-key vocal (C♯ → C major) sits over F–Am, which is IV–vi of the vocal's own key. The vocal's tonic chord never sounds, and its tonic note becomes the ♭3 of A minor and the 5th of F. "Lose It All" probably works the same way, with the major tonic B mapped to D, the ♭3 of B minor. Its chords are unsourced, so that part is unverified. This is consistent with the main report's finding that the loops avoid the tonic.
- If Chordify's 450 Hz reading is correct, "Clouds" sits about **+39 cents** above A440 (1200·log2(450/440) = 38.9). That would mean the vocal was not snapped to the grid and the instruments were tuned to the vocal. Unverified.

**C. Pitch placement for held vowels and sustained notes (*computed* from the sourced loops)**

| Loop (source) | Key | Chord tone in most chords | Consonant over *every* chord (universal set) | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VI–i (Clouds F–Am) | A minor | A (1) 2/2, C (♭3) 2/2 | {1, 2c, ♭3, 4, 5, ♭7} = minor pentatonic + 2 | ♭6 (F) over i |
| VI–iv–i (Lost My Mind F–Dm–Am) | A minor | **A (1) 3/3** | {1, 2c, ♭3, 4, 5, ♭7} | ♭6 over i |
| VI–VII–i–VII (Spaces, tab key: F–G–Am–G) | A minor (recording B♭ minor) | A (1) 2/4 + 9th over G | **{1, 2c, 4, 5, ♭7}** | **♭3 over VII** (C vs B), ♭6 over i |
| VImaj7–VII–i–VII (Moment A♭maj7–B♭–Cm–B♭) | C minor | C (1) and G (5) 2/4 each, 9th/6th over B♭ | {1, 2c, 4, 5, ♭7} | ♭3 (E♭) over VII, ♭6 over i |
| ii–IV–I–I (TRIPPIN Em–G–D–D) | D major | **D (1) 3/4 of loop time**, ♭7 over Em | {1, 2, 3, 5, 6} = major pentatonic (+7c) | 4 (G) over I; 1 over the verse's A/C♯ (V6) |

Rules derived from the table:
- **VI–i and VI–iv–i:** draw hook pitches from the minor pentatonic of i. A held vowel defaults to **1**, which is a chord tone in 100 % of chords; ♭3 is the second choice and also scores 100 % in VI–i.
- **Loops containing VII:** the universal set is {1, 2, 4, 5, ♭7}, the major pentatonic of ♭VII; 2 is only a #11 colour over VI. Default to **1** (a chord tone over VI and i, the 9th over VII), with 5 as the second choice. **Never sustain ♭3 into a VII bar.**
- **Major (TRIPPIN):** use the full major pentatonic. Default sustain is 1, but release or re-pitch it over A/C♯.
- **Generator constraint:** take one sustain pitch per hook cycle from the "chord tone in most chords" column, and allow at most one re-pitch per chord change. Under this rule a hook needs only **1–2 distinct pitches per loop cycle**, which matches the two syllables of "Clouds".

**D. Timing (*computed*)**

The main report puts the hook in the first bars, so the likely sequence is: stretched vowel first, ungated or lightly gated, then the full gate from the first drop at about bar 17–22.
- "Lose It All", 0:06 and 0:20 at 140 BPM: 3.5 and 11.7 bars have elapsed, a gap of 8.2 bars. The sampled phrase therefore repeats on an **8-bar cycle** and enters about 1–2 beats before the downbeats of bars 5 and 13, i.e. as a pickup. **E. Proposed default "BUNT. hook" generator (inference)**
- **Three hook types:**
  - (A) *Stretched vowel* ("Clouds").
  - (B) *Intact sung source phrase*, transposed by at most ±3 st and re-timed ("Lost My Mind", "Best Day", "Lose It All").
  - (C) *Featured topline plus a subdued stutter break* ("Other Side"; the main report lists Spaces and TRIPPIN as features).
- **Type A parameters:** 1–2 vowels, each held for at least 1 bar (based on "stretched as hell"; the exact length is unsourced). Pitches follow rule C. The gate defaults to 1/16 straight, with 1/32 on intensity bars (within Melodigging's 1/8–1/32 range). Retriggers follow 3+3+2 syncopation (steps 1, 4, 7, 9, 12, 15, as in the main report).
- **Section logic:** intro and verse hold the vowel without the gate. The drop uses the full gate plus retriggers. After the sung climax comes a "subdued stutter break", **with the kick still running** (per the "Other Side" review).

### Gaps

## 2. BUNT. "gleaming keys"/bright piano and the "staccato synth": rhythm, voicing, top note

### Takeaway
Only adjectives are sourced: "gleaming keys" that "thrum loudly" ("Clouds") and a "staccato synth" ("Peace In Silence"). Rhythm, voicing and top note are undocumented. The sourced chord loops do constrain the voice-leading tightly (*computed*):
- **VI–i** moves a single voice by one semitone (♭6→5) while 1 and ♭3 are held.
- **VImaj7** chords contain the whole i triad, so the keys can hold i while only the bass moves.
- **VI–VII–i–VII** forces parallel stepwise triads, giving a 4-chord arch in the top line.

### Inferences
- **Rhythm.** "Thrum" means a repeated, rhythmic sounding, and the review also mentions "oscillating beats". Together they suggest the keys are re-articulated rhythmically rather than held as one pad per chord. Proposed defaults (unsourced):
  - Keys: a sustained chord with a tempo-synced 1/16 gate in the drops, or repeated 8th hits, both under the sidechain pump.
  - "Staccato synth": 8th-offbeat stabs on steps 3/7/11/15, each note ≤ 1/16 long (≈ 115 ms at 130 BPM).
- **Programming.** Wijk composes in Ableton without a keyboard, so the parts are probably grid-quantised block chords at low velocity variance.
- **Voice-leading (*computed*; the voicings below are constructed, not transcribed):**
  - **VI–i (F–Am):** two common tones (A, C) and one mover, F→E (−1 st). There are three options:
    - Put the mover on top: F→E (♭6→5), an Aeolian "sigh".
    - Keep A (the tonic) on top and move the inner voice F→E.
    - **Use an i-triad pedal:** keep A-C-E in the keys and move the bass F→A. The result sounds like Fmaj7–Am. That maj7 brightness is one plausible reading of "gleaming"; this is speculative.
  - **VI–iv–i (F–Dm–Am):** A belongs to all three chords and can serve as the pedal on top or bottom. The movers are C→D→C and F→F→E.
  - **VI–VII–i–VII (F–G–Am–G):** adjacent chords share no tones, so the triads move in parallel. In one fixed inversion the top line is ♭3–4–5–4 (C–D–E–D), 1–2–♭3–2 (A–B–C–B) or ♭6–♭7–1–♭7. Steps are 1–2 st, the contour is an arch, and it repeats every 4 chords.
  - **VImaj7–VII–i–VII (A♭maj7–B♭–Cm–B♭, "Moment"):** A♭maj7 is the Cm triad over an A♭ bass, so **VImaj7→i changes only the bass**. Over B♭ the triad steps down (C-E♭-G → B♭-D-F), and the top line oscillates 5–4–5–4.
- **Top note.** Take it from the "chord tone in most chords" column in §1C: A or C in "Clouds", A in "Lost My Mind", A or E in "Spaces", C or G in "Moment", D in "TRIPPIN". Do not double the vocal's held pitch in the same octave; this follows the "bigger when there's less" principle.

## 3. BUNT. basslines: rhythm and note choice

### Takeaway
No source describes any BUNT. bassline. The evidence is indirect:
- genre copy ("bouncy, Eurodance-flavored", "syncopated basslines");
- remake genre labels (Future Garage for "Lost My Mind"; drum & bass for "TRIPPIN");
- the roots of the sourced loops.

The computed point: when the upper parts hold the common tones of a VI–i loop, the bass is the only voice that tells VI from i. A root-only bass is therefore functionally required.

### Cited Findings
- Stutter-house pack copy: "sliced, gated, and pitch-bent" vocals over "bouncy, Eurodance-flavored basslines and 4/4 drums with claps and hats". — [Rewind Samples](https://rewindsamples.gumroad.com/l/refract) (from research round 1)
- "Driving Stutter House" (references Fred again.. and Duskus & Bunt) promises "syncopated basslines"; its 87 loops are mostly bass and drums. — [BandLab](https://www.bandlab.com/sounds/pack/driving-stutter-house). MAGIX "Stutter House" contains 91 bass loops against 35 keys loops. — [Producer Loops](https://www.producerloops.com/Download-MAGIX-Stutter-House.html). Vandalism's "Shocking Stutter House" (Skrillex, Fred again.., Bunt) provides bass, lead and pad Serum presets. — [Producer Loops](https://www.producerloops.com/Download-Vandalism-Shocking-Stutter-House-For-Serum.html)
- An off-beat bass places its notes **between each kick**. Attack suggests starting from a sawtooth, sweeping the low-pass cutoff subtly downwards with the filter envelope, and using a slightly longer release. — [Attack Magazine](https://www.attackmagazine.com/technique/tutorials/low-end-theory-exploring-eight-common-bassline-styles/)
- A melodic-house bass is a syncopated line "that starts on the one, hits the groovy fourth-16th position, then an offbeat". It should "dance with the kick", sitting just below it. — [Production Music Live](https://www.productionmusiclive.com/blogs/news/melodic-house-basslines)
- The "Lost My Mind (Wake Up)" remake is sold as Future Garage, A minor, 2:27, built with Serum and Kickstart 2. — [TopMusicArts](https://topmusicarts.com/products/bunt-elley-duhe-lost-my-mind-wake-up-ableton-remake-dance). "TRIPPIN" is about 175 BPM drum & bass. — [Musicstax](https://musicstax.com/track/trippin/4ml1Eq4wmcdfN0Q6pNEly7)

### Inferences

- **Rhythm templates (16 steps; inference):**
