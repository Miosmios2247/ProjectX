## 1. Who is AVAION (background, instruments, singing, live setup), and which tracks are most popular or representative?

### Takeaway
AVAION (real name reportedly Christopher Stein, from southern Germany) is a self-producing singer, songwriter and producer. He writes, produces and sings his own songs. He usually starts a song from a guitar melody or a chord progression, and he plays piano, keys and guitar (organ as a child). He tours a live show in which he sings and plays MIDI instruments in real time on a 360° stage in the middle of the crowd. On Spotify his catalogue rests on five tracks: "Pieces" (2019), "Sleepless" (2022, Selected.), "WACUKA" (2025, with Sofiya Nzau), "Other Side" (2023, with BUNT.) and "Keep On Dancing". His second album *TO MAKE PEOPLE HAPPY* (16 Jan 2026) and "ifidiewouldyoucry" with BUNT. (17 Apr 2026) are the latest milestones.

#### Identity and background
- Beatportal profile: an electronic music artist from southern Germany, and the sole producer, singer and songwriter on his tracks. The tracks often set personal themes over uplifting dance beats. Releases shown include "To Make People Happy", "About You" and "Need Me". [Beatportal artist page](https://www.beatportal.com/artists/790778-avaion)
- Birth name Christopher Stein, born 9 January 1997 in "Furt", Germany. This comes from EverybodyWiki, a user-edited wiki, so reliability is low. [EverybodyWiki](https://en.everybodywiki.com/AVAION). Other sources give 1996 as the birth year, so the year is unverified. [Deezer](https://www.deezer.com/en/artist/14377487); [Beatport](https://www.beatport.com/artist/avaion/678255)
- He says his father and grandfather were musicians. He has a strong preference for guitar melodies but says he plays piano better. Interview statements; attribution not isolated among [Naluda Magazine](https://www.naludamagazine.com/interview-with-emerging-german-electronic-dj-and-producer-avaion/), [EDM Nomad](https://edmnomad.com/avaion-talks-about-his-music-career-and-his-future-plans/), [EDMunplugged 2021](https://edmunplugged.com/2021/01/19/avaion-in-an-exclusive-interview-discussing-his-music/) and [avaionmusic.com/history](https://avaionmusic.com/history/)
- Instrument history:

  - TIDAL bio: he played piano from age six, then moved on to guitar and keyboards. [TIDAL](https://tidal.com/artist/9715083)
  - Sleek: he started on piano, guitar and organ as a young child. [Sleek Magazine](https://www.sleek-mag.com/article/5-questions-to-avaion/)

#### Singing and songwriting process
- He sings on his own tracks: "I produce, sing and write all of my songs by myself". [The Student Pocket Guide, Aug 2025](https://www.thestudentpocketguide.com/2025/08/entertainment/music/avaion-interview/)
- Process: he usually begins with a guitar melody or a chord progression and quickly settles on the mood and subject. He then makes a rough recording to test how the vocals fit, because he doesn't consider himself a (trained) singer and sings in a higher range. Collaborations can take long; one took six months after a single day of initial work. [INJECTION interview (c. 2023)](https://www.injectionmag.com/post/interview-musician-producer-avaion)
- He describes his trademark sound as melancholic, blending house, electro, pop and acoustic elements. He also uses a self-described "AVAION style" mixing melancholic, upbeat, futuristic and acoustic elements. He has said he doesn't want to be "the depressed guy" who only writes sad songs, and points to more upbeat tracks such as "She Dance". Attribution not isolated among [EDMunplugged](https://edmunplugged.com/2021/01/19/avaion-in-an-exclusive-interview-discussing-his-music/), [EDM World Magazine](https://edmworldmagazine.com/say-his-name-and-dont-forget-it-avaion/) and [Naluda](https://www.naludamagazine.com/interview-with-emerging-german-electronic-dj-and-producer-avaion/)
- "Pieces" was created during a difficult, depressing period, while he was working late shifts and making tracks at his computer. [Beatport artist bio](https://www.beatport.com/artist/avaion/678255); [Frontview](https://frontview-magazine.be/en/news/avaion-re-relaese-his-platinum-selling-single-pieces-with-vize-and-leony-on-this-2021-version) (attribution not isolated)

## 2. BPM and key of the representative tracks

### Takeaway
Before 2023 the tracks sit at 120–126 BPM; from "Other Side" (Dec 2023) on they sit at 130–133 BPM. WACUKA is 130 BPM in the A-minor/C-major pitch collection. The keys are mostly sharp keys (E major, B minor, F♯ minor, D major), and the signature tracks are major-key more often than the "minor melodic house" cliché suggests. Several tools disagree only between relative keys, i.e. the same pitch collection.

- **The pattern across the transcriptions.** All of them are subsets of the pop-diatonic set {I, ii, IV, V, vi}. iii is never present, and in the minor-centred tracks the relative-major tonic (D in When You Go, C in WACUKA) is also absent.
  - Minor-centred AVAION loops therefore look like VI–VII–i–iv permutations, with the minor iv as the darker colour. The cliché i–VI–III–VII is wrong in exactly the III chord.
  - Major-centred tracks (Pieces, and probably Other Side in D, ifidiewouldyoucry in E, COLORS in G♭) use I–V–ii–IV-type loops. ii replaces the more common vi, which gives a hopeful, not gloomy, colour under melancholic lyrics.
- **Chord colour.** maj7 appears on the VI chord in When You Go and probably in WACUKA. Otherwise triads. The 2025–26 press stresses "lush chord stacks" and "bass chords", which suggests wide, layered voicings and possibly chords doubled in the bass register. This is an inference from marketing copy.
- **Harmonic rhythm and loop length** are not documented. Four-chord sets at 120–133 BPM in house usually mean 1 chord per bar in a 4-bar loop, or 2 bars per chord in breakdowns. Treat this as a default to test by ear, not a sourced fact.
- **Voicing medium.** Guitar-first writing and the "melancholic key medley" intro of WACUKA suggest piano or electric-piano style intros and breakdowns that become synth chord stacks in the drops. This is an inference.

### Takeaway
The only tool he has confirmed himself is Xfer Serum (WACUKA lead: trumpet-like patch, noise component, LFO automation inside Serum). ### Inferences
- Sound priorities for a generator patch set:
  1. A monophonic brass/trumpet-like Serum-style lead: saw or brass wavetable, filter, a noise layer, and LFO movement on filter or amplitude.

  3. Sampled, natural-sounding drums.
  4. Pink-noise risers and sweeps into drops.
  5. Sidechain pump on pads, chords and bass.
  6. OTT-style multiband compression on synths (community practice).
- Early-era tracks (2019–2023) were likely more "in-the-box" Serum deep house. The 2025–26 press deliberately stresses analogue warmth as the evolution. This is an inference from the timing of the analogue claims.

### Inferences
**Programmable as MIDI or parameters (sourced values in brackets):**
- **Tempo:** early 120–126, current 130–133. WACUKA is 130. [Q2]
- **Key pool:** E major, B minor, F♯ minor, D major, A minor/C major, G minor, A♭ minor, F♯/G♭ major. [Q2]
